How Nightfinder calculates the sky

Charts run from local noon to the following noon. Dark intervals are calculated from the geometric Sun altitude crossing −18°. The Moon and planets use topocentric positions. Deep sky targets use J2000 equatorial coordinates transformed to the date of your observation. Target windows and peak times use samples every six minutes.

Framing uses the specified sensor and focal length with a rectilinear lens. Catalogued sizes are approximate and assume you can turn the camera. Obstructions use your supplied profile; narrow buildings and short gaps can fall between six-minute samples. Times are approximate and no atmospheric refraction is applied. Check terrain, light pollution and cloud separately before travelling.
